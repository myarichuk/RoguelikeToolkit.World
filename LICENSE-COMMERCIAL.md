RoguelikeToolkit.World is dual-licensed:

| Use | License | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Personal projects, hobby games, study, research, evaluation, and use by charities, educational institutions, public research and government bodies | [PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0](LICENSE) | Free |
| Anything with a commercial purpose: games or tools you sell, monetize (ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, crowdfunding rewards), or use inside a for-profit company's products or services | Commercial license | Paid, by agreement |

The PolyForm terms decide what counts as noncommercial. Personal use with no anticipated commercial
application is free, even if you share the result. If you are unsure whether your use is commercial,
ask before you ship.

## Getting a commercial license

Open an issue titled **"Commercial license"** at
<https://github.com/myarichuk/RoguelikeToolkit.World/issues> or contact
[@myarichuk](https://github.com/myarichuk) on GitHub. Include your company or project, what you are
building, and how you plan to distribute it. Terms (per-title, per-seat or perpetual) are agreed case
by case. A commercial license is granted only by a written agreement signed by the copyright holder.

under MIT. The dual license applies to this version and later.
