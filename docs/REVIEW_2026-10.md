## 2. Should fix (wrong behaviour, narrower blast radius)

**Hydrology / climate**
- **Below-sea-level inland basins are treated as ocean** (`Hydrography.ComputeFilledSurface:148`, `HydrologyStage.cs:188`, `ComputeMaritimeAir`, `OceanCurrents.Solve:211`). Each is an outlet, a moisture source and a current cell, so a hyperarid Dead-Sea depression ends up filled to sea level and humid. Fix: flood-fill ocean connectivity from the largest marine body and route landlocked sub-sea basins through `LakeSolver`. *V*
- **Open-lake evaporation is only subtracted on the outlet path** (`HydrologyStage.cs:157`). Flat lake tiles next to the pour point bypass the `evapSink`, so outflow is overestimated. *V*
- **Glacier melt creates water** (`Climate/Glaciology.cs:100,111,126`). It adds a flat +2.0 runoff per glacier tile regardless of temperature, so frozen caps at T≈0 become major river sources. Scale by ablation (T above the snowline) or cap at accumulation. *V*
- **Temperature is clamped to [0,1] and stored clamped** (`ClimateStage.cs:233,325,339`). −10 °C and −50 °C are indistinguishable, and sea ice and glacier gates operate on saturated values. This is also a blocker for weather (see `WEATHER_PLAN.md` W0). *V*
- **`RotationRate` is applied inconsistently.**
  - Wind cell parity flips with the knob: rotation 2 or 4 puts a westerly at the pole, 1.5 does not.
  - `ZonalFactor` keeps a single ITCZ / dry-belt / storm-track triple, compressed equatorward, so polar dryness starts at 16° on fast spinners.
  - The Stommel boundary width ignores rotation.

  Fix: derive the zonal bands from the same cell index as the wind, and key polar dryness on temperature. *V*
- **`TidallyLockedInsolation` only changes temperature.** Wind, rain bands, seasonality, currents and the ≥82° glacier gate stay latitude-driven. Either build circulation around the substellar point or document the limitation. *V*
- **Wind is a step function by default** (`WindCellBlend = 0`): it reverses within one tile at 30°/60° at unit speed. *V*
- **Registered-but-unrun `ClimateInfo` looks like real data** to hydrology, erosion, biomes and `SeaIce`, so every land tile becomes glacier. This is the mirror image of the E1 bug. Share a "layer ran" check. *P*
- **Smaller climate and hydrology issues:**
  - Rivers that dry out mid-course are reported as `Terminal=Sink` while draining to the sea; add `DriesOut`. *V*
  - `RainSeasonality`/`RainPhase` sample `ZonalFactor` above 90° without reflecting. *V*
  - Ocean wind stress ignores the configured wind. *V*
  - Ocean-heat Jacobi is under-converged for the fastest currents. *P*
- **Elevation:**
  - `land = Continentality > 0` is inconsistent with `CrustThreshold = 0.12` (`ElevationGenerationStage.cs:109`). *V*
  - Hotspot latitude is drawn uniformly, not area-uniformly, which biases hotspots poleward. *V*

**Hierarchy / queries**
- **Region rivers ignore the parent's real flow direction.** `ParentContext.FlowEntryTile`, `FlowExitTile`, `Wind` and `NeighborHeights` are computed and never read (`ChildGridBuilder.cs:305-309,367-377`), contradicting `docs/API.md:401`. River channels don't line up across neighbouring regions. *V*
- **Local maps do not tile their region** (`RegionMaps.cs:168`): radius `R/size` against a cell pitch of `2R/(size−1)` leaves ~12.5% gaps along u and overlaps along v. *P (design)*
- **`GetRegion` throws `IndexOutOfRange` without tectonics** (`TerrainOrientation.cs:164`: `PlateInfo[Id−1]` with `Id == 0`). *V*
- **`|lat| > 90` returns a wrong tile instead of throwing** (`TileGrid.cs:341-343`): the row clamps but the vector wraps (probe: size 3 at (200, 10) → tile 89 vs exact 352). **NaN** centres or radii make `QueryRadius` return *every* tile, and `Nearest*` return null, while `GetTileIndex` throws on the same input. *V (probe)*
- **Local drainage loses flow** when a flat cell or sink spills into an already-processed higher neighbour (`ChildGridBuilder.cs:476-493`). *V*
- **`TerrainOrientation.Sample` scans the whole hydrology layer per `GetRegion` call** (164k tiles at size 7), and dominant-biome ties go by `Dictionary` order. *V*

**History**
- **Displaced people from a collapse or absorption are always written off as lost.** The pool group keeps the dead parent's id, and asylum skips dead polities (`HistoryDriver.cs:279,340,540`, `SettleOperator.cs:71`). *V*
- **Collapse successors keep their seat's old role** (Colony, Shrine, ...), so their "capital" can be captured or razed and gets no tier floor (`HistoryDriver.cs:559-562`). *V (probe: 6/30)*
- **`SuccessorCount` counts rebels** because M3.18 also sets `Parent` (`HistoryCatalog.cs:400`, `CollapseOperator.cs:115`). *V*
- **Narrative events commit even if their structural proposal was rejected** (`WarOperator.cs:160-162`; same for Rebellion and Villain). *V*
- **Commit order is by EntityId first**, so cross-polity conflicts (capture vs raze vs sack) are systematically won by low ids. It is deterministic but biased; document it or resolve conflicts by a seeded or priority key. *V*
- **Two routes built in the same turn drop shared segments.** `AddRoad.ConflictKey` is per tile pair (`Proposal.cs:53`), so the second route is stored with gaps. *V*
- **The treaty operator logs spurious "refused:foster" grudges** for pairs that married earlier the same turn (`TreatyOperator.cs:69-71`). *V*
- **A dead victor still imposes a conquest overlay** (`ConquestOperator.cs:41`). *V*
- **`RulerGeneration` counts from turn 0, not `FoundedTurn`.** *V*
- **`PopulationGrid.Step` with a duplicate cell in the subset underflows `uint`**, giving a population of about 4 billion (`PopulationGrid.cs:115,156`). *V*
- **Myths can be dropped by the per-turn event budget** (`FaithPostPass.cs:47`; use `AppendPriorityEvent`). *P*
- **Same-turn absorb/collapse of a parent that also spawned a rebel** can give two capitals on one tile, and `CapitalSettlements.Add` never checks for duplicates. *P*
- **`EntityStore` deltas can apply across forked chains**: `Load` restores the chain id and sequence, so a stale delta applies cleanly to a different base. Put a base-state hash in each delta. *P*
- **Culture-sensitive string ops:**
  - `NameText.cs:129-135`: `StartsWith` without `Ordinal`.
  - `NameGenerator.cs:91`: `CurrentCultureIgnoreCase`.
  - `OrderBy(n => n)` on load order in `PeoplesStore`, `NameDataStore` and `LocaleStore`.

  A Czech or Welsh machine loads differently. *V*
- **`Ethnos.Resolve` drops `FormAffinity`**, and a `default(CultureDelta)` throws a NullReferenceException. *V*

**Presentation / App / editor**
- **Selection highlight never shows on flat maps**: the uniform is the 3D sphere position, while `aPos` is the projected (x, y, 0). *V*
- **Date-line and pole artifacts on flat maps.**
  - Seam triangles are NaN-hidden, leaving a jagged hole along ±180°.
  - Pole vertices get longitude 0, producing slivers.
  - Mercator's pole tile spikes to y≈5.4.

  *V*
- **Gnomonic projection explodes near the horizon**, and faces with partial NaN vertices go to the GPU. *V*
- **Hex mode interpolates tile colours across triangles**, so tiles render as gradients rather than flat fills. Pick the arg-max barycentric corner in the fragment shader. *V (design; matters for the Unity port)*
- **Height slider at 0 still shows full relief** (`TerrainShading.DisplacedRadius` treats ≤0 as default). *V*
- **Minor rendering issues:**
  - Palettes pass NaN through.
  - `HistorySummary.ToJson` throws on non-finite plugin scalars.
  - `FlatHexLayout.HitTest` drills into edge cells from the empty margin.
  - The GLES shader requires `OES_standard_derivatives` unconditionally.
  - The worker hard-codes `HOLY_FOUND = 11`.

  *V*

## 3. API contract drift (docs promise X, code does Y)

- `AddStage` docs say duplicate orders throw at `Build()`. Only `Discover` checks, and the built-in Erosion, IslandChain and ClimateRefresh stages already share order 17. User stages at the same order run in insertion order. *V*
- `WithPlateCount(0)` is documented as valid, but it throws `InvalidOperationException` from tectonics, and that path triggers the leak in #10. *V*
- `WithPlanet` is silently ignored with `WithoutDefaultStages()`, and `World.Planet` then reports Earth. `World.Planet` can also drift from `ClimateStage.Planet`, whose setter is public. *V*
- `Discover` claims to find `IDeclaredStage` types without the attribute; it filters them out. *V*
- `WithElevation` replaces earlier configure callbacks instead of chaining them like the other `With*` methods. *V*
- `HistoryContext.TurnCount` doc says "0..TurnCount"; the driver runs 1..TurnCount−1. `StandardHistory` says eleven operators; there are 15. `NameDataStore` documents subfolders that the csproj does not embed. The history README says `Respatialize` is idempotent, which #4 disproves. *V*
- README: the Unity manifest snippet pins `0.3.0`, but the packages are 3.1.0 (release-please does not touch the README). `Glaciology.cs:51` says "sea ice is not modeled" though `SeaIce` exists. "Mass-conserving erosion" is broken by height pins. *V*
- The "zero-GC" claim does not hold for `Recompute` and `RegenerateHistory`: they rebuild clear-lists via reflection per call, and `IDeclaredStage` getters allocate. *V*

## 4. Incomplete functionality

- **Climate:**
  - No time axis (annual mean plus three seasonal scalars).
  - No pressure field.
  - Wind is prescribed, not derived.
  - Precipitation is a dimensionless reach index, not a conserved moisture budget.
  - Lakes never feed climate moisture in the default pipeline.
  - `ClimateStage.PrevailingWindFallback` is public and never read.
  - `PlanetParameters` has no radius, eccentricity, day length or atmosphere.

  → `WEATHER_PLAN.md`.
- **Hierarchy:** `ParentContext` flow/wind/neighbour fields are unused. `World.GetRegion`/`GetLocal` take no `RegionDetailOptions`. File-backed stores must live under the CWD (`ResolveStorePath`), which rules out Unity `persistentDataPath`.
- **History** (most are known milestones):
  - `MaxResourceEra` is not read.
  - `PeoplesStore.ExploitsOf` and `PairingProblem` are unwired.
  - M5.2–M5.6 are not started.
  - Tier hysteresis has no dwell time.
  - Settlement owners exist only as end state in the timeline.
  - Displaced groups and roads of dead polities are never cleaned up.
  - Sites are filled only post-run, so `TurnView.HasSite` is always false during simulation and every site gets `FoundedTurn = 0`.
- **Unity:** no runtime code at all. The packages are prebuilt DLLs + `link.xml`; Presentation is not netstandard and not packaged. → `UNITY_PLAN.md`.
- **Performance cliffs** (fine at today's caps, superlinear later):
  - `TradeRoadOperator.FindRoute`: unbounded planet-sized Dijkstra, failures never memoized.
  - `HistoricSiteIndex.FromContext`: roughly tiles × sites.
  - Quadratic scans in `HistoryDriver.Commit`.

## 5. Checked and sound

- **Topology and spatial lookups:**
  - `TileGrid.NearestTile` equals the exhaustive scan on 300k in-range points per size, sizes 0–7.
  - `GridCellAt` ≡ `GridCellAtExact`.
  - Icosphere edge keys are unique through level 9.
- **Wind and ocean-current signs:** trade-wind and Coriolis-turn signs per hemisphere and cell are correct. Ocean currents (Stommel discretisation, curl, β>0 in both hemispheres, gyre senses, western intensification) are correct.
- **Hydrology and erosion:** priority flood and flat resolution are deterministic. The glacial `ApplyMoves` dictionary use is order-independent.
- **Storage and RNG:** `EntityKeyMap` backward-shift delete, the free-list and slot validation are correct. `HistoryRng` uses FNV, not `string.GetHashCode`.
- **Editor interop:** the WASM interop strides match on both sides (polity 8, settlement 9, road 7, events 8+2·parts).
